# Injectables: proposed decisions on the 91 ST and IN controls

- **Purpose:** one table for the owner's single approval (plan section 9, Q1). After approval, the lead or owner records each row as that control's `expert_decision`; the build edits no control file.
- **Decision rule:** no binding source → DONT_FOLLOW; binding source → MODIFY (reduce to the plan's minimum, section 4); FOLLOW only where section 4 already builds the whole behaviour.
- **Binding:** numbered requirements (limit tables included) of Annex 1 (A1, EU-market batches only), Revised Schedule M (SM, always), EU GMP, 21 CFR 211 (US-market batches) and Part 11. Not binding: FDA-AP, PDA, ISO, PIC/S, and examples or guidance values the binding text names.
- **Sources** below are the clause numbers in each control's `sources:` field, minus the guidance ones. "(inferred)" marks a clause the control itself says it inferred.
- **Plan references** are rows of plan section 4 (4.1 to 4.6) and section 8a; "gate" = the existing release gate; "Qualif." = a kernel qualification type.
- **Date:** 4 November 2026. Prepared in slice I-0 from plan v2 (`docs/build-plan/injectables/plan.md`).

## Rows where the proposal is uncertain

- **ST-69:** Annex 1 10.7 and 10.10 permit early release; they do not require it. Read as "no binding duty", so DONT_FOLLOW. A strict reading of the rule ("binding source → MODIFY") would give MODIFY with an empty build.
- **ST-12, ST-13:** section 4 builds nothing for headcount or behaviour observation. The minimum may be nothing beyond the signer roster and terminal registration. The owner may want a NUM "people present" with a formulation maximum (A1 7.1).
- **ST-73:** section 4 builds no export or dashboard. MODIFY rests on block data being structured already.
- **ST-10:** its two existing tests are about the QP register, not cleanroom access. The tags may be wrong; worth a check by the lead.
- **ST-01:** the kernel already implements and tests the site-value floor table. MODIFY must not remove that tested behaviour; it only limits new sterile entries to binding numbers.
- **IN-42:** Schedule M labels Table 3 as averages for information. Under Q0, limit tables count, so it is treated as binding; judging each result singly is a design choice.
- **IN-51:** Part XIII lists recommended areas and equipment. Whether those lists are binding requirements or recommendations is unclear; minimum areas are not enforced in any case.
- **ST-25 to ST-27:** their sources are Annex 1 only, so they bind EU-market batches. For India-only batches the plan relies on SM 5.9 (water), which these controls do not cite.
- **ST-61:** parametric release waits for Q5 (Annex 17 check); the decision for that part may change then.
