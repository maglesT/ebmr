## Product description (from the product video and the owner)

Learnova ("Learn. Comply. Grow Together." — governed learning and SOP management for regulated teams) by Medhanta
Digipartner. Four roles: Admin, Faculty, Employee, Manager. Login by email and a one-time code. Admin: onboards
employees singly or by CSV (builds the reporting hierarchy), outside faculty, one toggle gives faculty access,
categories, employee groups, organisation chart, workflow oversight, delegation of curricula, settings, reset demo
data. Faculty: create curricula and SOPs (write the content or request it from a peer faculty member), SOP requests,
question bank, assessment questions per SOP, peer review before finalising (each reviewer sees only their own comments;
the owner sees all), assignment to groups or individuals. Employee: assigned training on the dashboard with due dates
and progress, sequential modules (each unlocks after the previous; video needs 90 % watched), assessments with a pass
mark and unlimited attempts, AI assistant available while learning but off during assessments, screenshots blocked;
on passing, a certificate with a unique number and a QR code, under "My Certificates", and a "Verify a Certificate"
page. Manager: team overview of the whole reporting line (completion rate, overdue, time taken per group), org chart,
team report, and their own training. Also notifications and AI-assisted learning. Internal demo portal:
https://lms.taxizee.com/ (demo mode).
